# The gates of Icecrown Citadel are about to open. 


The campaign against the Lich King has reached its final and most dangerous stage. Beyond the frozen walls of Icecrown Citadel, the greatest servants of the Scourge stand ready, and at the summit of the fortress, the Lich King awaits those brave enough to challenge his reign.
We are excited to announce that Icecrown Citadel Normal will open on September 5nd at 14:00 server time, unlocking the next major raid tier on Onyxia. There will be no downtime, the raid will become instantly available at the time above.
Icecrown Citadel Heroic will remain closed until a week later. September 12th at 14:00.
Gather your trusted allies, prepare your strongest equipment, and ready yourselves for the assault on the heart of the Scourge.


## Content release
Onyxia will progress into its next content tier with this raid release, unlocking the new vendors, instances, and relevant content automatically.
The content will include Icecrown Citadel, related dungeons (The Forge of Souls, Pit of Saron, and Halls of Reflection), and Vault of Archavon (Toravon).
Adventurers will be able to fight their way through Icecrown Citadel, confronting some of the Lich King's most powerful servants before ultimately ascending to the Frozen Throne itself.
The Realm First! Fall of the Lich King achievement will be awarded to the first group on the realm to defeat the Lich King on 25-player Heroic difficulty.
The [Strength of Wrynn] and [Hellscream's Warsong] buffs will remain on 0% until the realm first has been cleared, after which it will increase by 5% every week.

More details and information regarding the length of the content tier and the next content to be released will be published in a new thread.
